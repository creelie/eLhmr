\textbf{Cover letter prepared for Mathematics of Computation}

27 October 2026

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript \emph{A sixteen-prime-factor bound for Lehmer's totient problem and exact prime-completion criteria}, by Deep Bhattacharjee.
It gives a computational sixteen-prime-factor lower bound in Lehmer's
totient problem, strengthened bounds in the stated quotient cases,
and a finite classification for the companion divisibility with at most
eight prime factors. The manuscript separates the written reductions,
recorded exhaustive computations, independent certificates and partial
formal verification.

This revision gives detailed exact prime-completion criteria, diagrams,
and a proof that deletion of the largest prime cannot preserve a
hypothetical Lehmer counterexample. The full Lehmer conjecture remains
unproved. The submission therefore concerns the stated bounds and
classifications, not a claimed resolution of that conjecture.

The original preprint is
\url{https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00454v1}. The earlier confirmed research
archive is \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23638948}. Code and
verification records are available at \url{https://github.com/creelie/eLhmr}.
AI assistance is disclosed in the manuscript and in the accompanying
author-prepared declaration.
