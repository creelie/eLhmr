Dear Editors,

Please consider “Lehmer's totient problem with fewer than sixteen prime
factors” by Deep Bhattacharjee.

The manuscript gives a computer-assisted exclusion of composite solutions of
phi(n) | n-1 with at most fifteen distinct prime factors, further bounds for
large quotients and for solutions divisible by three, a size bound, and results
for the companion equation phi(n) | n+1. The reductions to finite searches
are proved in the text; code, recorded data and independent implementations
are available in the cited archive. The revision also proves that a fixed
prime prefix has at most one candidate for the largest prime and identifies
the remaining all-prefix assertion. It does not claim a solution of the full
Lehmer totient conjecture.

The research revision is archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23617604 .
The original preprint is https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00454v1 . Its replacement
version identifier will be added after the author supplies it. The same
mathematical manuscript is being prepared for the independent aiXiv archive;
an archive posting is not represented as journal acceptance. Earlier related
code archives, including the three-creator archive, are cited explicitly.

The manuscript identifies the locations of AI contributions in its declaration
before the bibliography. The article uses aomart without margin line numbers.
