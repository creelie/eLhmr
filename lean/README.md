# Lean formalisation

Lean 4 proofs of the lemmas, propositions and structure theorems of the paper
*Lehmer's totient problem with fewer than sixteen prime factors*. The exhaustive searches are not
formalised; the table of files below lists what is.

## Building

The project pins Lean `v4.34.1` (`lean-toolchain`) and Mathlib `v4.34.1` (`lakefile.toml`,
`lake-manifest.json`). With [elan](https://github.com/leanprover/elan) installed:

```
lake exe cache get      # optional: downloads compiled Mathlib
lake build              # builds LehmerTotient and everything it imports
lake env lean Check.lean
```

`Check.lean` prints the axioms of the main theorems. Each one must report only axioms among
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`: no `sorryAx`, and no `Lean.ofReduceBool` (finite checks use
`decide +kernel`, never `native_decide`).

## Contents

| module | paper |
|---|---|
| `Basic.lean` | Lemma 2.1 (i), (ii); Lemma 2.2, residues modulo 3 |
| `Search.lean` | `n/φ(n) = ∏ p/(p−1)` for squarefree `n`; Propositions 3.1, 3.2, 3.5 and the converse of 3.5; the form of 3.5 used in Appendix A.4 |
| `Three.lean` | Proposition 4.1 and its converse; Lemma 4.2; Section 4.4 (the sum identity, its converse, `c³ < N + 2c²`); `gcd(r, c) = 1` from the congruence prune |
| `FirstHit.lean` | Lemma 4.3 for the recursive function: termination, the least solution, and completeness of `none` |
| `Data.lean` | generated lists of primes in progressions, with a prime divisor of every skipped member |
| `Thresholds.lean` | Lemma 2.1 (iii); the thresholds 1540, 32 and 7: Lemmas 2.2, 2.3, Corollary 2.4; the bounds 33 and 1540 for a quotient at least 3 that the products give (Theorem 1.5 improves them with the search of Proposition 2.5) |
| `ExtBasic.lean` | Theorem 1.6 (i), (ii); equation (7.1); Remark 7.1; Proposition 9.1 (pseudo-solutions); Lucas certificates |
| `Ext.lean` | Theorem 1.6 (iii): the closure of 1 under the two extensions is exactly the eight Fermat-type solutions |
| `Barrier.lean` | Lemma 9.2 (the number of entries divisible by 3) and the fact that `gcd(x_i, x_j − 1) = 1` follows from the product equation; Proposition 9.4 for prime-power moduli (Lemma 2.2 is the only congruence obstruction) |
| `LehmerExt.lean` | Proposition 9.6: two entries `A + 1 + d`, `A + 1 + e` with `d e = A² + A − 1` turn a solution with ε = +1 into one with ε = −1; pairwise coprimality holds exactly when `gcd(d² − 1, A) = gcd(d − 1, A + 2) = 1`, which fails when `5 ∣ A` (quadratic reciprocity) |
| `PseudoExtend.lean` | Theorem 9.5: from a companion pseudo-solution prime to 3 with `3 ∣ ∏ (xᵢ − 1)`, appending `2∏(xᵢ − 1) + 1` gives another one, and appending `∏ xᵢ` gives a Lehmer pseudo-solution prime to 3 |
| `PseudoData.lean` | Theorem 9.5: a companion pseudo-solution prime to 3 with 25 entries, checked by the kernel; pseudo-solutions prime to 3 for every `k ≥ 25` (companion) and every `k ≥ 26` (Lehmer) |
| `Eight.lean` | Section 7.4: the reduction to `n + 1 = 2φ(n)` with `p₁ = 3` (given `3 ∣ n`); step (i), `c(A'p + ε) = A't + N` and `t ∣ A'p + ε ↔ t ∣ N` for `gcd(t, c) = 1`; step (ii), equation (7.2), the class and range of `σ` and the converse; step (iii), the sieve keeps every value from integers at no excluded residue, which holds for integers without prime factors up to 61 and, in the search for `k = 13` of Theorem 9.3, for every completion prime to 3 |
| `EightData.lean` | the 21 completions `s < p < q` of the eight-prime search, each with the equation and a proper divisor of `p` or `q` checked by the kernel |
| `Product.lean` | Section 6.1: the identity (6.1), Lemma 6.3, Lemma 6.4 with its strict form, the extremal sequence and identity (6.3), Lemma 6.5 (Cook, Nielsen), Theorem 6.2 and its equality case when `d ∣ b + 1`; Section 6.2: Proposition 6.8 and Corollary 6.10, so that a solution that is not `s`-heavy has `n < 2^(2^(k−s))` |
| `Structure.lean` | Section 9.7: the product equation has degree one in each entry, so a prefix has at most one completion; Proposition 9.7 (i) and (ii); Lemma 9.8, and Korselt's criterion for pairwise coprime entries with ε = −1 |
| `Largest.lean` | Section 9.8: Lemma 9.9 (the defect is prime to 2AB, the last entry X and X − 1 are prime to 2B and to A, the defect 1 gives X = A + 1 + ε, and the behaviour modulo 3 for Lehmer's sign) and Lemma 9.11 (the defects of the children of a prefix) |

`Data.lean` and `Ext.lean` are generated by `scripts/gen_data.py` and `scripts/gen_ext.py` (Python 3 with
`sympy`), `PseudoData.lean` by `scripts/gen_pseudo.py` from `pseudo/companion_k25.txt`, and `EightData.lean` by
`scripts/gen_eight.py` from the journal `data/companion8/journal.jsonl`; running any of these
scripts rewrites its file with identical content.
